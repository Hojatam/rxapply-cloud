# RxApply · 23 employees · face briefs for ChatGPT Image

The 23 agents in our control plane act like a real team of employees. We want to give each of them a face — a realistic, warm, professional portrait — to humanize the dashboard. The brief below is for ChatGPT Image (GPT-Image-1) or any photorealistic model.

**Final deliverable**: a single composite image — a 5×5 grid (with the bottom-right two slots reserved for the RxApply wordmark and the brand color square) — containing all 23 portraits. Same lighting, same camera distance, same crop, same background gradient. They must look like they were photographed by the same photographer in the same hour.

---

## 1 · Master prompt (paste this around the per-agent briefs)

> A single high-resolution composite image showing **23 photorealistic professional headshots** of the RxApply AI team, arranged in a clean **5-column × 5-row grid** (the last two slots in the bottom-right are decorative — see below). Same studio shoot for everyone:
>
> - **Crop**: head + shoulders, centered, ~70% headroom.
> - **Camera**: 85mm portrait lens, soft focus on background, sharp on eyes.
> - **Lighting**: large softbox key from upper-left, gentle fill from right, warm temperature (~3200K).
> - **Background**: identical for everyone — a smooth gradient from warm cream (`#f8fafc`) at top-left to soft slate (`#e2e8f0`) at bottom-right. No props, no logos behind subjects.
> - **Style**: editorial portrait photography, natural skin texture (not over-airbrushed), slight smile or neutral confident expression, eyes direct to camera (or 3/4 turn for a few).
> - **Wardrobe palette across the team**: muted indigo, deep navy, warm beige, charcoal, soft cream, terracotta accents. No loud branding. Everyone looks like they work in a thoughtful professional services firm — calm, hype-free, not corporate-sterile.
> - **Diversity**: a real-feeling international team. Predominantly Middle Eastern / Persian / South Asian / Levantine descent (the brand serves dentists migrating from those regions), with a few European and East Asian colleagues. Ages **25–35**.
> - **Below each portrait**: a thin 1px line, then the agent's first name in **lowercase Inter font**, ~12pt, slate gray (`#475569`). Below the name in even smaller text, the one-line role.
>
> **Bottom-right two grid slots** (positions 24 and 25) are NOT portraits. They contain:
> - Position 24: the **RxApply wordmark** in indigo (`#4f46e5`) on the same gradient background, with a small tagline below: *"23 employees · one calm dashboard"*.
> - Position 25: a flat indigo (`#4f46e5`) square with the text "rxapply.com" in white, centered.
>
> Render at **2400 × 3000 px** so each portrait is ~480 × 600 px individually. Make sure no two adjacent portraits feel visually identical — vary skin tone, hair, glasses, and wardrobe across rows.

## 4 · Visual cohesion notes

Things to keep consistent across all 23 portraits — these are what make it feel like one shoot:

1. **Eye level matches** — every face's eye line is at roughly the same height in its grid cell.
2. **Same focal length feel** — no fish-eye distortion on close-ups; everyone shot at 85mm-equivalent.
3. **Same key light direction** — soft from upper-left, gentle shadow on right side of face.
4. **Same background gradient** — cream top-left → slate bottom-right, identical for every cell.
5. **Wardrobe limited to a 6-color palette** — muted indigo, deep navy, warm beige, charcoal, soft cream, terracotta. No bright reds, no neon, no patterns louder than a subtle texture (one mustard outfit allowed for ramin to keep the team from looking sterile).
6. **No costume props** — no stethoscopes, no laptops, no headsets in the portrait crop. The brief's a *team photo*, not role-play.
7. **Subtle skin texture** — natural pores, soft asymmetries. No plastic-perfect skin retouching.
8. **One unbroken team** — no random distinctly-different lighting on any portrait.

---

## 5 · Things to AVOID in the brief

- **No exaggerated youth filters** — these are 25-35-year-old professionals, not Instagram avatars.
- **No corporate-stock-photo grin** — the brand is hype-free; smiles are mild and real, never "thumbs-up energy".
- **No ethnic stereotype shorthand** — give people specific features, not generic "Middle Eastern man".
- **No traditional clothing** unless it'd be normal in a Tehran or Toronto coworking space (so a soft scarf is OK; full ceremonial dress is not).
- **No babies, no children, no anyone under 22.**
- **No celebrity look-alikes** — every face should feel new.
- **No surreal elements** — no glowing eyes, no AI/cyberpunk overlays. These are humans.
- **No cropped-out hair / cropped-out chin** in the head + shoulders frame.

## 6 · How to use this brief with ChatGPT Image / GPT-Image-1

The image-generation model has prompt limits, so feed the brief in **two passes**:

**Pass 1 — generate the empty grid** with the master prompt from §1, asking for "23 placeholder portraits in a 5×5 grid, plus 2 brand cells, on the cream→slate gradient background. Maintain the lighting, camera, palette, and label-line style described."

**Pass 2 — replace each cell** with one face from §3, one at a time or four at a time, using the per-agent brief. The model will keep the global lighting/background coherent if you reuse the master prompt header each time.

If the model can't reliably hold cohesion across 23 cells, do **6 portraits at a time as 2×3 mini-grids**, then composite them in Figma later. Same shoot description for each batch.
